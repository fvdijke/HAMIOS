# HAMIOS v5.8.3 — Propagation overview is back

**Release Date:** September 30, 2026
**Build:** PyInstaller 6.x | Python 3.10

---

## 🧭 Propagation overview

The card-style advice panel from before v5.7 is back, as an extra panel next to **Propagation Advice** (the recommendations). You find it as a tab beside Propagation Advice; drag it out by its tab to give it its own place, or switch it on and off in the Panels menu.

Eleven fixed cards:
- Best bands · Geomagnetic conditions · Solar activity · Solar wind and Bz · Flares
- Time of day · Mode / power · Auroral absorption · Sporadic-E · DX routes · Overall conditions

Improvements over the old panel:
- Band percentages come from the same calibrated model as **Bands now** (the old panel used a rough formula of its own)
- Day or night follows the real sun at your QTH
- Every card is always present (with a "calm" text when nothing is going on), so the grid no longer jumps
- A card whose content changes shows a **blinking dot for 5 minutes**, however often the panel refreshes; no dots at start-up
- The number of columns follows the panel width (1–3); in a low panel the cards scroll instead of being squeezed

## 🗂 Tab groups

A tab group with only one visible panel now shows that panel as a normal panel with its own title bar; the tabs come back when a second panel is shown again.
